About

A name that carries the meaning of courage

Al-Karrar is the one who charges the objective and does not turn back; the arrow is precision in hitting it. From those two ideas came the name and the mark: a falcon, an arrow and a shield, standing for steady direction, strong protection and fast execution.

Conviction

We choose our direction carefully, then hold it until the result.

Precision

Measured quality at every stage, from sourcing to handover.

Commitment

Clear dates and measurable promises for every client and partner.
